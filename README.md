# Supplier Rebate Accrual Recovery

Calculate earned supplier rebates and recover missing accruals, claims, credits, and cash.

**Primary buyer:** Manufacturers, distributors, and procurement teams. **Evidence:** supplier agreements, purchases, eligible products, tiers, growth targets, mix incentives, exclusions, accruals, claims, credits, and payments.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Supplier agreement library
- Eligible product registry
- Purchase transaction ingestion
- Net purchase calculation
- Return exclusion control
- Volume tier calculation
- Growth baseline calculation
- Product mix incentive
- Customer project exclusion
- Rebate accrual ledger
- Claim preparation
- Supplier statement audit
- Dispute workflow
- Credit cash reconciliation
- Program supplier analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4702>. API: `5702`.

Administrator: `runtime-admin@example.com` / `LocalDemo!2026`. Operator and reviewer credential buttons are available on the login page.
